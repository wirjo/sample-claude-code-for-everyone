Q2 2025 Go-to-Market Strategy
March 2025

Executive Summary:
We're launching the new dashboard platform in Q2 with a goal of acquiring 50 new enterprise customers and expanding 20 existing accounts. Focus on financial services and healthcare verticals where we have proven success. Key differentiator is real-time analytics with customizable dashboards.

Target Market:
- Mid-market companies (500-5000 employees)
- Primary verticals: Financial services (40%), Healthcare (30%), Technology (30%)
- Geographic focus: US and Canada, expanding to UK in Q3
- Buyer personas: VP Engineering, CTO, Data Analytics Managers

Competitive Landscape:
- Competitor A: Market leader, strong brand but expensive and rigid
- Competitor B: Growing fast, good UX but limited enterprise features
- Competitor C: Full-featured but complex and slow to implement
- Our position: Fast implementation, flexible customization, competitive pricing

Value Proposition:
"Get enterprise-grade analytics that your team can customize and deploy in days, not months, at half the cost of traditional solutions."

Key Messages:
1. Speed: Deploy in 2 weeks vs 3 months with competitors
2. Flexibility: No-code customization - no engineering required
3. Performance: Sub-second query times even with millions of records
4. Cost: 50% lower TCO than enterprise alternatives

Campaign Strategy:

- Launch blog series: "Analytics for Fast-Moving Teams"
- Create 5 customer case studies (TechStart, Acme Corp, others)
- Develop comparison guides vs top 3 competitors
- Produce video tutorials and demo library

Demand Generation:
- LinkedIn campaign targeting VP Engineering, CTOs ($15K budget)
- Google Search ads for high-intent keywords ($10K budget)
- Webinar series: "Modern Analytics Stack" (3 sessions in Q2)
- Participate in 2 industry conferences (FinTech Summit, Healthcare IT)

Sales Enablement:
- Updated pitch deck with new dashboard screenshots
- ROI calculator tool for sales team
- Competitive battlecards for top 3 competitors
- Customer reference program (recruit 10 reference customers)

Partner Channel:
- Recruit 5 implementation partners

Product Marketing:
